01 · 2024—now · Co-founder, Head of Product & Technology

Sidekick

A master teacher, in every tutoring session.

Step Up pairs under-resourced students with volunteer tutors who drive 2–4 additional months of learning. Sidekick is the AI copilot that makes every one of those sessions better: it analyzes session recordings, briefs the tutor in one minute, diagnoses what the student knows, and generates the right next lesson.

  • Watches every session: the recording (student work, dialogue, the tutor's own teaching moves) becomes a one-minute briefing before the next one.
  • Coaches the coach with research-backed feedback on teaching moves like wait time, praise, and talk moves, plus suggested phrasing to try next session.
  • A knowledge graph of skills spots prerequisite gaps, generates scaffolded practice with talk-moves baked in, and tracks mastery across sessions.

2–4 extra months of learning/1,000s of tutoring hours a week

The Step Up efficiency engine

Six years of automating a 20-person org, from Zapier to AI agents.

Step Up runs on systems I built: Airtable and Bubble wired through Zapier, webhooks, and custom APIs into every app we use, automating communication, onboarding, and backend data management. It runs 200,000+ automations a month for 6,000+ active users, and workflows that started on Zapier are steadily becoming AI agents.

  • Built a workflow auditor, a Gemini tool that logs how the team works and flags what to automate next; its successor turns those workflows into Claude agent skills.
  • Built the Work Tracker: per-person workstreams assembled automatically from PRs, tickets, docs, and meetings, with a weekly AI team summary posted for the whole org so everyone stays aligned.
  • Product feedback closes its own loop: tutors tag Claude on feedback and it ships preview builds and PRs for review.
  • Onboarding portal used by roughly 600 tutor applicants a week; replacing the application's video screener with an automated interview system took completion from 10% to 60%.
  • Tutor dashboard auto-connects tutors and parents over SMS and web, with automatic translation.
  • Growth ops that worked: an ambassador program with automated referral tracking drove 1,000+ tutor applications.
  • Own the 5-year LAUSD MOU and funder relationships: Gates Foundation, CZI, NewSchools.

Neat Freak

Tidy your tabs. Find your work.

A Chrome extension that snapshots every open tab into memory-light sessions, auto-organized into folders by workstream, then finds anything again with plain-English search. Live on the Chrome Web Store. Privacy-first: no account, no server, no telemetry.

  • Gentle, proactive nudges: when open tabs cross your threshold it offers to tidy, already knowing which tabs you're done with. Ignore it and it backs off.
  • Local association-graph clustering, with an optional LLM pass for workstream-aware folder names.
  • Natural-language search over every saved tab ("ux applicants I looked up?"), ranked by an LLM.
  • Full product surface shipped solo: MV3 service worker, onboarding, manager UI, store listing.

Chrome MV3 · JavaScript · LLM search · local-first
