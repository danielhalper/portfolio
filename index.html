Sidekick — Step Up Tutoring

A master teacher, in every tutoring session.

Step Up pairs under-resourced students with volunteer tutors who drive 2–4 additional months of learning. Sidekick is the AI copilot I lead product and engineering for: it analyzes session recordings, briefs the tutor in one minute, diagnoses what the student knows, and generates the right next lesson.

  • Watches every session — analyzes the recording (student work, dialogue, the tutor's own teaching moves) into a one-minute briefing before the next one.
  • Coaches the coach: research-backed feedback on teaching moves — wait time, praise, talk moves — with suggested phrasing to try next session.
  • Spots prerequisite gaps, generates scaffolded practice with talk-moves baked in, and tracks skill mastery across sessions.
  • Tutors tag Claude on product feedback and it ships preview builds + PRs; support runs on Zendesk + AI triage.
  • Lead the 9-person team; manage the 5-year LAUSD MOU and funders: Gates Foundation, CZI, NewSchools.

300,000+ tutoring hours/1,000s of weekly pairs/5-yr LAUSD MOU
