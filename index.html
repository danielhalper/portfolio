About

The through-line: I see what's needed, design the answer, and build it myself. I studied Product Design at Stanford, then added full-stack engineering at Hack Reactor, finishing top of my cohort.

At Step Up I've spent six years turning a scrappy, manual operation into AI-powered systems for matching, support, feedback, and data, while keeping the humans (tutors, students, families) at the center. That pattern is what I love most: take something people do by hand, understand it deeply, and productize it with AI.
